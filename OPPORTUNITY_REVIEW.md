# Reviewing opportunities

The dashboard offers an optional, browser-local review of saved opportunities. This is a user-controlled organizing aid, not a guarantee of qualification or a prediction of career outcomes.

When evaluating an internship, examine the position's responsibilities, research methods, mentorship, team, expected ownership, timing, and eligibility. The employer's posting is the authoritative source.

User-entered preferences and notes are saved locally in the browser and are not included in generated GitHub listings. Keep exported tracking backups in a private location.
