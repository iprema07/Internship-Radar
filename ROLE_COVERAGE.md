# Role discovery: what gets tracked

The job scanner is **statistics-first**, not restricted to a single employer type. It searches employer ATS data and public internship sources, then scores the *work described* in each opening. This is a rule-based filter, not a guarantee of qualification.

### Statistics & methods (top priority)

Statistician, Biostatistics, Statistical Programming, Clinical Statistics, Statistical Scientist, Statistics & Decision Sciences, Biometrics (when actually statistical), Experimentation, Causal Inference, Survey Methodology, Statistical Computing, psychometrics and econometrics.

### Closely related areas

Epidemiology, RWE, HEOR, outcomes research, statistical analysis of trials and clinical data, bioinformatics, computational biology, data science, ML research and product/clinical/business analytics. General data engineering and analytical consulting are secondary.

### Discovery technique

1. Scan employer job boards and public sources across industries.
2. Match clear statistics-focused titles, or detect multiple statistical methods in a generic research title or a statistics-related department.
3. Avoid generic SWE, financial trading, wet-lab-only and unambiguously doctoral-exclusive roles.
4. Separate role relevance from compensation, season confirmation and admissions/degree eligibility.
5. Publish a grouped [OPENINGS.md](OPENINGS.md) listing with a direct apply link and an interactive dashboard.

### Limitations

Some Workday or iCIMS postings do not provide reliable public feeds. Relevant programs can appear in the dashboard's manual watchlist. Job-board coverage is incomplete; upstream data may lag, and generic internships sometimes need human review. Employer postings determine actual eligibility, salary and availability.
